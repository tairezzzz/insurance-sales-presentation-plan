Underwriting

From the Word guide to rules the AI can cite

Carrier guide, section 4.2: heart conditions Word

¶1Applicants who have had a heart attack, stent placement or bypass surgery within the past 24 months are not eligible for any plan.

¶2If the event was more than 24 months ago and the applicant has not been hospitalized in the past 12 months, offer the Graded benefit plan.

¶3Current use of nitroglycerin for chest pain requires the Guaranteed issue plan, regardless of the event date.

Recommendation shown to the agent AI explanation

Carrier B, Graded benefit. Heart attack 30 months ago, outside the 24-month window, and no hospital stay in the past year, so rule HC-02 (§4.2 ¶2) applies.

Check The medication list mentions nitroglycerin. If it is still in use, rule HC-04 moves this client to Guaranteed issue. Confirm with the client.

Agent override

The agent can pick a different eligible option and must record why. The reason, the rule version and the AI suggestion are stored together in the audit log.

Eligibility always comes from the published rules. The AI only ranks and explains what the rules allow.

20-day plan

Five milestones, one accountable developer from prototype to deployment

Days 1-3

Architecture and prototype

  • Custom, low-code and existing-platform comparison
  • Architecture, data flow, security
  • Clickable prototype and final plan
Days 4-8

Presentation and admin

  • Responsive slides and branching flow
  • Dromo launch token and prefill
  • Coverage and needs analysis
  • Admin for slides, questions, priorities
Days 9-13

Underwriting and AI

  • Word rules into decision tables
  • Conditional questionnaire
  • Eligibility, ranking, explanations
  • Override workflow
Days 14-17

Quotes and One Fill

  • Quote API integration
  • Filter and rank up to five
  • Embed One Fill and pass all data
Days 18-20

Testing and deployment

  • Dromo write-back and activity tracking
  • Laptop, tablet, phone and browser tests
  • Offline save and sync tests
  • Deploy to your cloud, code and docs

What you own

Your code, your cloud, open-source parts

Source code

Everything lives in your repository from day one, with documentation at handover.

Your cloud account

The app, the database and the files run in your account under your access controls.

Open-source parts

The CMS and the rules engine are open source, so no contractor-controlled dependency sits in the stack.

Rules you control

Admins upload a new Word guide, review the drafted changes and publish a new version.

Security

Launch token checked on the server, health data encrypted at rest, every access logged.

After launch

An optional monthly maintenance plan covers fixes, rule updates and carrier changes.
